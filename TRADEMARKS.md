# TRADEMARKS — Kioku

The AGPL-3.0 license (see `LICENSE`) grants rights to the **code**. It
does not grant rights to the **"Kioku" name or logo** — consistent with
AGPL §7(e), which lets a publisher decline trademark grants. This file
is the trademark policy that makes that declination explicit.

## Ownership and status

- The "Kioku" name, any logo, and derived marks ("the Marks") belong to
  the Kioku project and its founder.
- Status as of 2026-10-23: **no trademark registrations exist yet**. This
  policy is effective as a promise of how the project intends to act,
  whether or not registration has happened. Registration is planned
  before the hosted launch (own cost line — not a blocker for self-host).

## What you MAY do without asking

- **Refer** to Kioku truthfully: "MyCompany runs Kioku", "X is a plugin
  for Kioku", " банкed on Kioku: <link to upstream>" — nominative fair
  use for identification is fine everywhere.
- **Self-host and run** unmodified Kioku for yourself or your team,
  including branding pages as "Kioku" verbatim.
- **Contribute** back; the contribution license you grant doesn't touch
  The Marks.

## What you may NOT do

- Market or distribute a **modified version under the plain "Kioku"
  name or logo** in a way that makes its origin confusing — forks must
  rename, or clearly and prominently mark themselves as unofficial:
  "Tuned Kioku — community fork, not affiliated with the Kioku project".
- Register domains, social handles, apps, or company names whose
  primary value is confusion with Kioku or that imply project
  endorsement that doesn't exist.
- Use The Marks as part of your own product name ("KiokuPRO",
  "Kioku Notes by You").
- Sublicense, transfer, or convey any rights in The Marks you were
  never given.

## Fork/derivative works (AGPL compliance is separate)

You always may fork, modify, and redistribute under AGPL-3.0 — that's
the license. The fork just can't present itself AS Kioku. Renaming plus
a truth line ("based on Kioku, <upstream link>") satisfies both.
